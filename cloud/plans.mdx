Every plan includes every feature — the agent, the API and MCP, webhooks,
analytics, client groups, automations, all of it. Tiers differ only in
**channels, AI credits and seats**. Posts are unlimited everywhere.

| AI videos / month | — | 20 | 60 | 120 | Custom |

Current pricing is on the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/en/pricing.html).

## How limits behave

**Channels** are counted as connected, including disabled ones. Over the limit,
connecting another is blocked until you disconnect one.

**Monthly allowances** reset on the billing date, not the first of the month.
Exhausting them stops new posts of that type; already-scheduled posts still
publish.

## Changing plan

Upgrades apply immediately. Downgrades apply at the end of the current period,
so you keep what you paid for.

<Warning>
Downgrading below your current channel count does not disconnect channels
automatically. You choose which to remove — until you do, publishing to the
excess is blocked.
</Warning>

## Trial

The trial runs for seven days with a card on file. It converts automatically at
the end unless cancelled. See [billing](/cloud/subscription).
