**Settings** in the sidebar, organised as tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Account** | Your name, email, password, language |
| **Team** | Members and roles — see [team members](/general/settings/team) |
| **Channels** | Every connection in one list, with reconnect and delete |
| **Sets & signatures** | Reusable channel selections and sign-offs — see [reusables](/general/settings/reusables) |
| **Developers** | API keys and OAuth apps — see [developer settings](/general/settings/developers) |
| **Billing** | Plan, payment method, invoices — see [subscription](/cloud/subscription) |

## Why a tab might be missing

Tabs follow role and plan. Team members below admin do not see Billing or
Developers, and features a plan does not include are hidden rather than shown
disabled. If a tab you expect is absent, check your role first —
see [team members](/general/settings/team).
