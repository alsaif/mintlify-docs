An honest list, maintained as things change.

## Platform-side constraints

- **TikTok visibility follows the selected audience.** Nushir's production app
  is approved for Direct Post, but a saved Self only post remains private.
  Choose from the creator's current permitted privacy options. An inbox upload
  still needs completion in TikTok; a successful private post is not public.
- **Instagram Stories cannot carry all composer features** — Stories accept
  media plus limited options, by Instagram's design.
- **Reddit moderation is invisible to the API.** A post can be published
  successfully and removed by subreddit moderators moments later; Nushir still
  shows it as published because, from the API's view, it was.

## Product limitations

- **Analytics backfill is shallow** — metrics collection starts when a channel
  is connected; history from before then is not imported.
- **Moving a published post edits the record, not the platform** — see
  [moving posts](/general/calendar/moving-posts).
