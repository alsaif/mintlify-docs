## The two facts that resolve most tickets

**Account types gate publishing.** Instagram requires Business or Creator;
Facebook requires a Page; several platforms restrict by account kind. A channel
that connects but cannot publish is almost always this — see
[platform reference](/general/platforms/overview).

**Tokens expire by design.** Platforms cut tokens on a schedule, and
immediately when you change your password there. Reconnecting from the channel
menu is routine maintenance, not a fault.
