---
title: "Third-party integrations"
description: "Pull generated media from external services into your posts."
---

The **Integrations** section connects external content services, so their
output can be attached to posts straight from the composer.

## How it works

Connect a service with its API key. Its generated media then appears as a
source in the composer's media picker, alongside the
[media library](/general/media-library).

## Which services

The list depends on what the deployment has enabled — open **Integrations** in
the sidebar to see what your account offers. Services that are not configured
do not appear at all.

<Note>
These integrations *bring media in*. For pushing events out to your own systems
when posts publish, you want [webhooks](/general/automations/webhooks).
</Note>
