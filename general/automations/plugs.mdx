---
title: "Plugs"
description: "Automatic follow-up actions on posts that perform."
---

A plug watches a published post and acts when it crosses a threshold.

## What they do

**Auto-repost** — publish the post again after it passes a threshold, to reach
people who missed it.

**Auto-comment** — add a prepared comment once a post performs. Often used to
put the link in a comment rather than the post body, where a platform penalises
outbound links.

## Setting one up

**Plugs** in the sidebar, choose the channel, set the threshold and the action.
Plugs are per channel, so a rule for one account does not silently apply to
another.

## Worth knowing

<Warning>
Thresholds depend on the platform reporting the metric. On a platform that
reports nothing, a plug will never fire — see [analytics](/general/analytics) for
which platforms report what.
</Warning>

Reposting the same content repeatedly is treated as spam on several platforms.
Auto-repost is safest used sparingly and on platforms where reposting is normal.
