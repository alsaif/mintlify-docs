Open any post on the calendar for its action menu.

## Duplicate

Copies the post — content, media, channels and settings — as a new draft. The
fastest way to run a variation of something that worked.

## Preview

Renders the post as each platform will display it, and produces the shareable
link used for [client approval](/general/approvals).

## Delete

Removes one post, or the whole cross-channel group.

<Warning>
Deleting a published post removes Nushir's record only. The post stays live on
the platform — no platform allows deletion through a scheduling API.
</Warning>

## Statistics

Once published, the post shows the metrics its platform reports — views,
likes, comments, shares — refreshed on the platform's own schedule.

Coverage is uneven by platform: see [analytics](/general/analytics) for which
platforms report what. A platform that reports nothing shows an empty panel,
not zeros.
