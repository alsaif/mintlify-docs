| Analytics | None. |

## Connecting

**Add Channel → Discord**, approve access on the platform, and you are returned
here. See [connect a channel](/general/channels/connect) for the general flow.

## Worth knowing

Publishes to a channel in a server, not to a personal profile.

## Posting over the API

Use `discord` as the channel's `identifier`. The `settings` object this platform
expects is listed in [channel settings](/public-api/channel-settings).
