## Connecting

**Add Channel → TikTok**, approve access on the platform, and you are returned
here. See [connect a channel](/general/channels/connect) for the general flow.

## Worth knowing

`content_posting_method` selects the flow: `DIRECT_POST` submits for direct
publication; `UPLOAD` sends a draft to the creator's inbox to finish in TikTok.
An inbox upload is not a published post.

Nushir's production app is approved for Direct Post. Choose an audience from the
creator's current permitted options; these can include Public, Friends or Self
only. Availability still depends on the connected creator. A saved Self only
post stays private unless you explicitly change its audience. Confirm the exact
post's settings and TikTok's applicable music-use acknowledgment before sending.
Nushir reports publication only after TikTok confirms completion.

## Posting over the API

Use `tiktok` as the channel's `identifier`. The `settings` object this platform
expects is listed in [channel settings](/public-api/channel-settings).
