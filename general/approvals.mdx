---
title: "Review and approve posts"
description: "Approve a draft, request changes with a note, or cancel it while keeping its history."
---

Open a post in the calendar to see its full caption, media, schedule and review
history. Workspace admins can review a draft directly.

## Review actions

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve & schedule** | Validates the draft and schedules it for its future publish time. An admin does not need to request approval first. |
| **Request changes** | Requires a note explaining what to fix, up to 500 characters. The post stays a draft and the feedback stays on the post. |
| **Cancel post** | Keeps a canceled draft, its content and review history. It will not publish. A cancellation note is optional. |

Cancellation here applies to a draft. Queued and published posts retain their
existing editing and scheduling controls.

## Complete settings before approval

If a TikTok draft has missing or invalid publishing options, the review panel
lists them and disables approval. Choose **Complete TikTok settings** to finish
the existing form inside the review panel. Select the audience and posting
method yourself and confirm the displayed consent. The form checks current
creator permissions and keeps unsupported choices unavailable.

**Save settings** keeps the post as a draft and preserves its caption, media,
schedule and review history. Choose **Approve & schedule** separately when it
is ready. If the draft changed while you were editing, use **Refresh post** and
review that version before saving. Settings completion does not replace the
usual media and future-time checks.

## Make corrections and resubmit

A member can edit a returned draft and choose **Resubmit for review**. Choose a
future publish time first. It stays a draft until an admin approves it. An admin
can approve a corrected draft directly.

For a canceled draft, **Resubmit for review** reopens it. Previous feedback stays
in the review history, including after a new submission replaces the latest note.

## Who receives the feedback?

Nushir uses the submitting member or the trusted account that created the post.
If a shared API/MCP credential has no identifiable human submitter, the reviewer
chooses an active workspace member under **Send feedback to**. A shared token
does not identify a separate agent email address.

The recipient gets an in-app notification linking to the post. Review email
follows their **Settings → Global Settings → Post review emails** preference,
which is off by default. Publishing-success and publishing-failure email
preferences remain separate. Queued email is not a delivery receipt.

MCP clients can read `approvalStatus`, `approvalNote` and `reviewCanceledAt` with
Get Post Status or List Posts. They check feedback on demand; Nushir does not
wake a disconnected assistant. See [MCP review submissions](/mcp/introduction#request-review-and-handle-feedback).

## Shared preview comments

You can also share a post's preview link with a client. The link exposes that
post's content, media and target channels; the recipient can comment without
seeing the rest of your workspace. Private review notes and actor details stay
inside the workspace; they are excluded from public preview responses.

Public preview comments collect feedback. They do not perform the workspace
review actions above or stop a post that is already scheduled. Keep a post as a
draft when you need sign-off before scheduling it.
