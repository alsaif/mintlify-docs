Connecting a channel gives Nushir permission to publish content on your behalf and read your analytics. You can connect any of 30+ supported platforms, and each connection uses one slot from your plan's channel allowance. The process takes under two minutes per platform and can be reversed at any time.

## How to Connect a Channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open channel settings">
    Go to **Settings → Channels** and click **Add Channel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your platform">
    Choose the platform you want to connect from the list of supported channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Depending on the platform, you will either be redirected through an OAuth flow (most platforms) or asked to paste an API key or access token. Follow the on-screen prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Once authentication completes, the channel appears in your channel list with a green status indicator. It is immediately available for scheduling.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Permissions Nushir Requests

When you connect a channel, Nushir requests the minimum set of permissions needed to do its job:

- **Publish content** — to schedule and post on your behalf at the approved time.
- **Read analytics** — to let the Analyst agent read engagement data and improve your posting schedule.

Nushir does not request permissions to follow accounts, send messages, or take any action outside publishing and reading analytics. You can revoke these permissions at any time from the platform's own settings (for example, from Instagram's **Authorized Apps** page or Twitter/X's **Connected Apps** section). Revoking permissions on the platform side disconnects the channel in Nushir automatically.

## How Channel Slots Are Counted

One connected account equals one channel slot. The table below shows the limit per plan:

| Plan       | Channel Slots |
|------------|---------------|
| Solo       | 5             |
| Studio     | 15            |
| Agency     | 40            |
| Enterprise | Unlimited     |

If you manage multiple accounts on the same platform — for example, three separate Instagram business pages — each page counts as one channel. You can connect and disconnect channels freely at any time without losing your scheduled content.

<Note>
  **Add-on:** If you need more channels than your plan includes, you can add individual channel slots for SAR 12 (approx. $3.20) per channel per month on any plan.
</Note>

## Disconnecting a Channel

To disconnect a channel, go to **Settings → Channels**, click the channel you want to remove, and select **Disconnect**. The channel is removed from your list immediately.

<Note>
  Disconnecting a channel does **not** delete posts that were already scheduled for it. Those posts move to **Draft** status and remain in your calendar. You can reconnect the channel later or reassign the drafts to a different channel.
</Note>

## Swapping Channels

If you have reached your plan's channel limit and want to connect a new platform, disconnect an existing channel first to free up a slot, then connect the new one. No content is lost — the disconnected channel's posts move to Draft as described above.
