## Free Trial

Before you commit to any plan, you get **7 days of full Studio access** with 1 connected channel and no credit card required. Use the trial to explore all four AI agents, the approval workflow, the Calendar, and API access. At the end of the trial, choose a plan to continue — or your account pauses with no charge.

## Annual Billing

Choosing annual billing saves you **20%** compared to paying month-to-month. You can switch from monthly to annual billing at any time in **Settings → Billing** — the discount applies from the next billing date. Switching from annual back to monthly takes effect at the end of your current annual term.

## Upgrading and Downgrading

<Accordion title="Upgrading to a higher plan">
  Upgrades take effect immediately. You are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your current billing period at the new plan's rate. You gain access to the higher plan's features — additional channels, users, agents, and quotas — right away.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Downgrading to a lower plan">
  Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. You continue to use your current plan's features until the cycle ends. **No content is deleted** when you downgrade — posts, media, and analytics are preserved. If your current channel count exceeds the lower plan's limit, you will be prompted to disconnect channels before the downgrade is finalized.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cancelling your plan">
  You can cancel at any time from **Settings → Billing → Cancel Plan**. Access continues until the end of your paid period. After that, your account moves to a read-only state — you can export your content and data, but cannot publish or use AI agents. Content is retained for 90 days before deletion.
</Accordion>
