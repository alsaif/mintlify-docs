Nushir's team management lets you bring in collaborators with exactly the level of access they need — whether that's full editorial control or read-only comment access. Roles keep your content workflow clean, and the approval assignment system ensures the right person signs off before anything goes live.

## User Limits by Plan

| Plan | Included users |
|---|---|
| Solo | 1 (Owner only) |
| Studio | 3 |
| Agency | Unlimited |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

## Roles

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Owner" icon="crown">
    Full access to every feature including billing, plan changes, and account settings. There is one Owner per account, and the Owner role cannot be reassigned without contacting support.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Editor" icon="pen-to-square">
    Can create, edit, and approve posts; manage connected channels; and use all AI agents. Editors cannot access billing or remove team members.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reviewer" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Can view drafts and published posts, and leave comments. Reviewers cannot create posts, approve content, or publish — they are designed for clients or stakeholders who need visibility without publishing rights.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Invite a Team Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    Click the **Invite Member** button in the top-right corner of the Team page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email and select a role">
    Type the invitee's email address and choose their role: **Editor** or **Reviewer**. (The Owner role is not assignable through invitations.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Send Invite**. The invitee receives an email with a secure link. When they click the link, they are prompted to create a password (or sign in if they already have a Nushir account) and are added to your team immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Existing Members

To change a team member's role or remove them from the account:

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Click the team member's name or row to open their profile card.
3. Use the **Role** dropdown to change their access level, or click **Remove** to revoke their access entirely.

Removed members lose access immediately. Their login session is invalidated within a few minutes.

<Note>
  Removing a team member does **not** delete any posts they created. All drafts, approved posts, and published content they authored remain fully intact and are reassigned to the Owner for further management.
</Note>

## Approval Workflow (Studio & Agency)

On Studio and Agency plans, team collaboration extends into the content review process. Any Editor or Owner can assign a draft post to another team member for review:

1. Open a Draft post in the editor.
2. Click **Assign** and select a team member.
3. The assigned member receives an in-app notification and an email.
4. They open the post and can **Approve**, **Request Changes**, or **Reject** it.
5. If changes are requested, the original author receives a notification and can revise the post before it is reviewed again.

For a full walkthrough of the approval workflow, see [Review & Approval](/publishing/review-approval).
