When you run social media for multiple clients, keeping everything clean and separated is non-negotiable. Nushir's workspace feature gives each client their own isolated environment — their own channels, posts, media library, brand settings, and analytics — all managed from a single Agency account. You never have to worry about mixing up a client's content or accidentally sharing one client's data with another.

## Creating a Workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workspace settings">
    Go to **Settings → Workspaces**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new workspace">
    Click **New Workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the workspace">
    Enter the client's name or a recognizable internal label for the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**. The new workspace is immediately available in your workspace switcher with a clean slate — no channels connected yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Is Isolated Per Workspace

Each workspace is fully self-contained. Nothing crosses over between workspaces unless you explicitly share it:

| Item | Isolated per workspace |
|---|---|
| Connected channels | ✅ |
| Posts and drafts | ✅ |
| Media library | ✅ |
| Brand settings (logo, colors, fonts) | ✅ |
| Analytics and reports | ✅ |
| Monthly branded client reports | ✅ |

Your channel plan limit (40 channels on Agency) applies across all workspaces in total — not 40 per workspace.

## Team Access Across Workspaces

Team members can be invited to individual workspaces rather than your entire account. A team member can have different roles in different workspaces — for example, an Editor in one client's workspace and a Reviewer in another's.

To add a team member to a specific workspace:

1. Switch to the target workspace using the workspace switcher.
2. Go to **Settings → Team** within that workspace.
3. Invite or assign team members exactly as you would in the main account settings.

Members who are not invited to a workspace cannot see its channels, content, or analytics at all.

## Monthly Branded Reports

Each workspace automatically generates a **monthly performance report** styled in the client's brand identity — using the logo, colors, and fonts you set in that workspace's brand settings. The report covers channel growth, post performance, engagement trends, and AI-generated insights from the Analyst agent.

You can designate one or more email addresses to receive the report automatically at the end of each month. Set this up in **Settings → Reports → Auto-send** within the workspace.

## Switching Between Workspaces

Use the **workspace switcher** in the top-left corner of the app to move between clients. The entire interface — Calendar, Analytics, Channels, and Media — updates to reflect the selected workspace. Your current workspace is always visible in the switcher.

## Availability

Workspaces are available on **Agency** and **Enterprise** plans only. If you are on the Studio plan and need to manage multiple clients, consider upgrading from **Settings → Billing**.

<Tip>
  Before you start connecting channels or generating content in a new workspace, go to **Settings → Brand** inside that workspace and upload the client's logo, primary colors, and preferred font. This ensures that the monthly branded report is accurate from day one and that the Designer agent generates media that matches the client's visual identity automatically.
</Tip>
