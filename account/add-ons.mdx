If you hit the limits of your plan mid-month, you don't need to upgrade — add-ons let you extend exactly what you need, when you need it. You can add more connected channels on a recurring basis or top up your AI image and video generation credits for the current billing month.

## Available Add-Ons

| Add-on | Price | Billing |
|---|---|---|
| Extra channel | 12 SAR / month | Recurring — charged each billing cycle until removed |
| 100 extra AI images | 29 SAR | One-time — credits expire at end of current billing month |
| 10 extra AI videos | 49 SAR | One-time — credits expire at end of current billing month |

Extra channel slots are available on all plans. Image and video packs can be purchased as many times as you need within a single month — each purchase adds to your remaining credit balance.

## How to Purchase an Add-On

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    Go to **Settings → Billing → Add-Ons**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the add-on">
    Choose **Extra Channel**, **Image Pack (100)**, or **Video Pack (10)** from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the quantity">
    For channel slots, enter how many additional channels you want. For image and video packs, enter the number of packs to purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm purchase">
    Review the total and click **Confirm**. The additional channels or credits are available immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Expiry and Rollover Policy

Image and video packs are tied to your current billing month. Any unused credits from a pack **do not roll over** to the next month — they expire at midnight on the last day of your billing period.

Channel add-ons are recurring. They renew automatically each billing cycle at 12 SAR per channel per month. To remove an extra channel, disconnect it in **Settings → Channels** and then remove the add-on slot in **Settings → Billing → Add-Ons**.

<Note>
  When your included image or video quota runs out, generation pauses automatically — there is no surprise charge and no interruption to your publishing schedule. Scheduled and approved posts continue to go live as planned. When you're ready to generate more content, purchase a pack from **Settings → Billing → Add-Ons** and generation resumes immediately.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Video credits are best purchased just before a specific campaign rather than keeping them active every month. Because credits expire at month end, buying a 10-video pack when you have a concrete campaign planned ensures you use every credit before they reset — rather than buying speculatively and losing unused credits.
</Tip>
