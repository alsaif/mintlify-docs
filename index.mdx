## How Nushir Works

Nushir replaces the manual loop of writing, designing, and scheduling content with four agents that collaborate on your behalf. You set the goals and brand rules once — the agents take it from there.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your channels">
    Link your social accounts in one step. Each connected account counts as one channel in your plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Teach the agent your brand">
    Upload your tone of voice, brand colors, fonts, and a list of words or promises to avoid. You only do this once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve">
    The Planner builds a monthly calendar. The Writer fills it with copy. The Designer attaches media. You open the calendar, review drafts, and approve — or edit before publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn and improve">
    After posts go live, the Analyst reads the numbers, adjusts future timing and topics, and generates a branded monthly report automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>
