## What it does

**One calendar, every channel.** Write once and send it everywhere, or tailor the
wording per platform without maintaining several drafts.

**Scheduling that respects each platform.** Every channel carries its own posting
times and its own rules — character limits, media requirements, privacy options.
Nushir enforces them before you schedule, not after publishing fails.

**Arabic and English throughout.** The interface, the calendar and the composer
all run right-to-left when Arabic is selected.

## What it does not do

Worth knowing up front, because these are the things people expect and do not get:

- **Publishing is never guaranteed.** Nushir schedules and calls each platform's
  API. Platforms have outages, revoke tokens, and change their rules. A post can
  fail for reasons no scheduler controls.
- **Not every platform reports analytics.** Where a platform exposes no metrics
  API, Nushir shows nothing rather than inventing a number.
- **Deleting a published post in Nushir does not delete it from the platform.**
  It removes the record here only.
