Nushir is built around a simple principle: the AI agents propose, and you decide. Every post — whether drafted by the Planner agent or composed manually — starts as a **Draft** and stays there until a human explicitly approves it. This draft-first workflow gives you complete editorial control while still letting automation handle the volume.

## The Approval Workflow

The journey from idea to published post follows a clear path:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    The AI agent or you create the post. It sits in Draft status on your Calendar, visible but not scheduled to publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit (if needed)">
    Open the draft to review copy, check media, and confirm the scheduled time. Make any adjustments directly in the editor — changes save automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Click **Approve** when the post is ready. The status changes to Approved and the post is queued to publish at its scheduled time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Published">
    At the scheduled time, Nushir sends the post live. The status updates to Published and basic engagement data begins to flow in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reviewing a Draft

Click any **Draft** card in the Calendar to open it in the editor. Before approving, check:

- **Copy** — read the text carefully; click **Edit** to make changes inline
- **Media** — confirm the image or video is correct and properly cropped for the channel
- **Schedule time** — verify the date and time match your intended publish window
- **Channel** — confirm the right channel (or channels, for cross-posts) are selected

Once satisfied, click **Approve**. If the post needs significant work, click **Edit** to revise it, or click **Reject** to remove it from the queue entirely.

## Team Approval (Studio & Agency)

On Studio and Agency plans, you can distribute the review workload across your team:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assign a post for review">
    Open a Draft post in the editor and click **Assign**. Select a team member from the list. They will receive an in-app notification and an email alerting them that a post is waiting for their review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reviewer actions">
    The assigned team member opens the post and can take one of three actions:
    - **Approve** — marks the post ready to publish
    - **Request Changes** — adds a comment explaining what needs to change; the post loops back to the original author
    - **Reject** — removes the post from the queue with an optional reason
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revise and resubmit">
    If changes were requested, the original author receives a notification, revises the post in the editor, and resubmits it for review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Client Approval (Agency)

For agencies presenting work to clients, Nushir provides a frictionless review path that keeps final control in your team's hands:

1. Share a **read-only calendar link** with your client (generated from **Settings → Calendar → Share Link**).
2. The client views all scheduled posts and can leave **comments** on individual post cards.
3. Your team reads the client's comments and makes any agreed changes.
4. A team member gives the final **Approve** — clients can never publish directly.

## Auto-Publish

If you trust the agent's output on a particular channel and want to remove the manual approval step entirely, you can enable auto-publish per channel.

Navigate to **Settings → Channels → [channel name] → Auto-publish** and toggle it on. Posts scheduled for that channel will skip the Approval step and go live automatically at their scheduled time.

<Warning>
  Enable auto-publish only for channels where you have high confidence in the agent's output and where a mistaken post would have minimal consequence. We recommend keeping auto-publish off for any channel with a large audience or where brand tone is especially sensitive, until you have thoroughly reviewed several cycles of agent-generated content.
</Warning>

## Notification Settings

Stay on top of your review queue without constantly checking the Calendar. You can configure notifications at **Settings → Notifications**:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pending reviews" icon="bell">
    Get notified when a post is assigned to you or when a post enters Draft status on a channel you manage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Publish confirmations" icon="circle-check">
    Receive a confirmation each time a post goes live, so you always know when content has been published.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Publish failures" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Get an immediate alert if a post fails to publish, with a link directly to the failed post card so you can retry quickly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Change requests" icon="comment">
    Be notified when a reviewer requests changes on a post you authored, so revisions move forward without delays.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
