One strong idea shouldn't live on only one platform. Nushir's cross-posting feature lets you select multiple channels in a single draft and automatically adapts the copy and media for each one — so you reach every audience without rewriting the same post five times.

## How Cross-Posting Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Create a new post by clicking **+** on a Calendar slot, or open an existing draft. Both manual and agent-generated posts support cross-posting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select multiple channels">
    In the **channel selector**, choose two or more connected channels. You can mix platform types freely — for example, X, LinkedIn, and Instagram in a single post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the Writer agent adapt the copy">
    Once multiple channels are selected, the Writer agent automatically tailors the copy for each platform's conventions:
    - **X** — concise, punchy copy within the 280-character limit, with a strong hook
    - **LinkedIn** — longer-form narrative with professional tone, up to the platform's full post length
    - **TikTok** — a short spoken script designed for a 15-second video caption
    - **Instagram** — caption-forward copy with strategic hashtag placement
    You can review each channel's version by clicking the channel's tab in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve">
    Check each channel tab to confirm the adapted copy reads well. Make any edits you want on a per-channel basis, then click **Approve** once to queue the post across all selected channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Per-Channel Overrides

The adapted copy is a starting point, not a constraint. Click any channel's tab in the editor to override the text or swap the media for that specific channel. Your changes apply only to that channel — every other channel in the same post remains untouched.

This is useful when:

- A particular channel has a unique brand voice requirement
- You want to add a channel-specific call-to-action (e.g., "link in bio" for Instagram)
- The agent's adaptation needs a light edit to match your preferred tone

## Media Adaptation

The Designer agent automatically crops and resizes your media to match each platform's required aspect ratio:

| Platform | Ratio | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Feed | 1:1 | Square post |
| Instagram Reels / TikTok | 9:16 | Vertical video |
| YouTube | 16:9 | Horizontal video / thumbnail |
| LinkedIn / Facebook | 1.91:1 | Landscape image |
| X | 16:9 or 1:1 | Inline tweet image |

Upload one high-resolution source image or video and the Designer handles the rest. You can preview each crop in the channel's tab before approving.

## A Practical Example: Product Launch

To see cross-posting in action, consider a single "product launch" idea adapted across three channels:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="X (short & punchy)">
    ```
    Our new dashboard is here. Faster insights, cleaner design, zero learning curve.

    Try it free for 7 days → [link]

    #ProductLaunch #SaaS
    ```
    *280 characters. Strong hook, single link, two hashtags.*
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="LinkedIn (narrative)">
    ```
    Six months ago, our users told us the same thing: "The data is there, but I can't act on it fast enough."

    Today we're launching our redesigned analytics dashboard — built entirely around that feedback.

    Here's what changed, and why it matters for social teams managing high-volume publishing...

    [continues with three short paragraphs and a CTA]
    ```
    *Long-form narrative. Professional tone. Opens with a customer insight.*
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="TikTok (spoken script)">
    ```
    [0–3s] "If you've ever felt lost in your analytics…"
    [3–8s] "We just launched something for you."
    [8–13s] "One dashboard. All your channels. Instant clarity."
    [13–15s] "Free trial link in bio."
    ```
    *15-second script. Punchy sentences. Clear CTA for the bio link.*
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Each connected channel counts as one channel slot against your plan's limit, regardless of how many times you include it in a cross-post. Cross-posting to three channels in one draft uses three of your channel slots, not one. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing) for channel limits per plan.
</Note>
