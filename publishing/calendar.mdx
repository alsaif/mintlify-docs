The Nushir Calendar is the central hub where your entire content pipeline comes to life. Every post your AI agents draft, every piece you compose manually, and every scheduled publish appears here — giving you a single place to review, approve, and manage content before anything goes live.

## Calendar Views

The Calendar opens in **week view** by default, showing seven days at a glance with posts arranged in time slots. Switch to **month view** to get a higher-level picture of your publishing cadence. Use the toggle button in the top-right corner of the Calendar to flip between views at any time.

## Reading a Post Card

Each post appears as a card inside its scheduled time slot. At a glance, every card shows:

- **Channel icon** — the social platform the post is destined for
- **Status badge** — the current state of the post (see below)
- **Content preview** — the first line of copy or a thumbnail if the post is image-led

### Post Statuses

## Filtering the Calendar

When you're managing multiple channels or a heavy publishing schedule, filters help you zero in on what matters:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter by channel">
    Open the **Filters** panel above the calendar and select one or more connected channels. Only posts destined for those channels will appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by status">
    Choose one or more statuses — Draft, Approved, Published, or Failed — to focus your review queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by date range">
    Use the date range picker to narrow the calendar to a specific window, useful for auditing a past campaign or previewing an upcoming one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sharing the Calendar With Clients

If you need a client or stakeholder to view your publishing schedule without giving them an account, you can generate a read-only shareable link.

Navigate to **Settings → Calendar → Share Link** and click **Generate Link**. Anyone with the link can view the calendar and post cards, but cannot edit or approve anything. You can revoke and regenerate the link at any time from the same settings page.

<Note>
  Auto-publishing is an opt-in feature that lets approved posts go live without a final manual trigger. It is disabled by default on all channels. You can enable it per channel in **Settings → Channels → [channel name] → Auto-publish**. See the [Review & Approval](/publishing/review-approval) page for full details.
</Note>
