Whether you want the AI agents to do the heavy lifting or you prefer to write every word yourself, the Nushir editor gives you a fast, flexible workspace. Every post — regardless of how it was created — passes through the same editor before it ever reaches a channel.

## Two Ways to Create a Post

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="AI-generated from a plan">
    When the Planner agent builds your content calendar, it creates draft posts automatically. These drafts appear on your Calendar with a **Draft** status badge. Click any draft card to open it in the editor, review the copy and media, then approve or refine before it publishes.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Compose manually">
    Click the **+** button on any empty time slot in the Calendar to open a blank editor. You set the copy, media, channel, and schedule from scratch — useful for time-sensitive posts or one-off announcements that fall outside your regular plan.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Inside the Editor

The editor is divided into a set of clear, task-focused sections:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Text area">
    Write your post copy in the main text area. A **character counter** below the field updates in real time and reflects the limit for each selected channel, so you always know when you're approaching a platform's maximum.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Media attachment area">
    Drag and drop images or videos, or click **Upload** to browse your media library. The Designer agent can generate new media directly from this area — see AI Assist below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Channel selector">
    Pick one or more connected channels from the channel selector. Selecting multiple channels enables cross-posting; the Writer agent adapts the copy for each platform automatically. See [Cross-Posting](/publishing/cross-posting) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Per-channel override tab">
    When two or more channels are selected, each channel gets its own tab at the top of the editor. Click a channel's tab to customize the copy or swap the media specifically for that channel — without affecting the other channels in the same post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scheduling date/time picker">
    Set the exact date and time you want the post to go live. The picker displays times in your account's local timezone. You can also leave the time blank to save the post as an unscheduled draft.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## AI Assist: Write and Design on Demand

You don't have to leave the editor to call on the AI agents:

- **Write** — Click the **Write** button to have the Writer agent generate or rewrite the post copy based on your plan's topic, tone, and channel. You can run it multiple times to get different variations, then edit the result to your liking.
- **Design** — Click the **Design** button to have the Designer agent generate a visual for the post. Describe the image in the prompt field, or let the agent interpret the post copy automatically. The generated image lands directly in the media area.

## Saving and Approving

Posts save automatically as **Draft** every time you make a change — you will never lose work. When you're happy with the copy, media, and schedule, click **Approve** to mark the post as ready to publish. Approved posts will go live at the scheduled time without any further action on your part.

<Tip>
  Use per-channel override tabs when posting to platforms with very different format expectations. For example, an X post benefits from a punchy one-liner and a striking image, while the same idea on LinkedIn might deserve a three-paragraph story with a document attachment. Set the shared copy as your base, then tailor each channel's tab — you only need to approve the post once.
</Tip>
