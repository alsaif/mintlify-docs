Your best content has a shelf life far longer than its original publish date. Evergreen reposting lets you schedule any post to repeat at a set interval — weekly, monthly, quarterly, or on a custom cadence — so high-value content keeps reaching new audiences long after it was first published. The Analyst agent monitors engagement across your channels and automatically surfaces the posts most worth reposting, so you never have to hunt for candidates manually.

## What Evergreen Reposting Does

When you add a post to your Evergreen Queue, Nushir creates a fresh draft of that post at each interval you specify. The draft goes through the normal review-and-approve workflow before it publishes — which means you can update the copy, refresh the media, or approve it as-is. Nothing reposts automatically without your sign-off unless you have auto-publish enabled on the channel.

The Analyst agent continuously scores your published posts based on reach, engagement rate, saves, and shares. Posts that outperform your channel averages appear in the **Evergreen Suggestions** list for quick queuing.

## How to Enable Evergreen Reposting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a published post">
    In the Calendar, click any post with **Published** status to open it in the post detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Evergreen">
    Click **Add to Evergreen** in the post actions menu (top-right of the detail panel).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your interval">
    Choose how often you want the post to repeat:
    - **Weekly** — re-queued every 7 days
    - **Monthly** — re-queued on the same day each month
    - **Quarterly** — re-queued every 3 months
    - **Custom** — enter any number of days
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm**. The post is now in your Evergreen Queue and will appear as a Draft at the next scheduled interval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Analyst Recommendations

You don't have to browse your entire post history to find evergreen candidates. The Analyst agent does that work for you:

1. Go to the **Analytics** tab in the main navigation.
2. Open the **Evergreen Suggestions** section.
3. Browse the list of posts the Analyst recommends based on engagement performance.
4. Click **Add** next to any post to add it to the Evergreen Queue and set an interval.

## Editing Before a Repost Goes Live

When an evergreen interval triggers, Nushir creates a new Draft — it does not republish the original post verbatim. Open the draft from your Calendar before approving it. You can:

- Refresh the copy to reflect current context
- Swap the media for an updated visual
- Adjust the scheduled time to suit the current content calendar
- Approve it unchanged if the original is still fully relevant

## Pausing or Removing Evergreen Entries

To manage your active evergreen schedule, go to **Publishing → Evergreen**. The Evergreen Queue lists every post that is set to repeat, along with its interval and next scheduled date.

- **Pause** — toggle off any entry to suspend it without deleting it; toggle back on to resume
- **Delete** — remove an entry permanently from the queue; this does not delete the original published post

<Tip>
  Seasonal content is a perfect use case for an annual evergreen cadence. Set a Ramadan campaign post, a Saudi National Day message, or a Founding Day piece to repeat once per year. The system queues a fresh draft each year so you can update it for the current context before it goes live — no need to rebuild from scratch.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Evergreen reposting is available on **Studio** and **Agency** plans. If you are on the Solo plan and want access, you can upgrade at any time from **Settings → Billing**.
</Note>
