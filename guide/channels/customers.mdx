---
title: "Client groups"
description: "Group channels per client and filter the calendar to one at a time."
---

An agency running ten clients has a calendar that is unreadable by default.
Client groups fix that.

## Grouping channels

Assign a channel to a client from its menu. A channel belongs to at most one
client group.

## Filtering

Select a client above the calendar and everything narrows to their channels —
the calendar, the composer's channel list, and analytics.

## Over the API

`GET /groups` lists them, and both post listing and creation accept a `customer`
parameter to scope the request:

```bash
curl "https://platform.nushir.com/api/public/v1/posts?startDate=2026-09-01T00:00:00Z&endDate=2026-09-30T23:59:59Z&customer=CLIENT_ID" \
  -H "Authorization: $NUSHIR_API_KEY"
```
