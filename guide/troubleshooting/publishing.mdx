---
title: "A post did not publish"
description: "Reading the failure, and what to do about it."
---

Open the post. A failed post keeps the platform's own error message, which is
usually more specific than anything Nushir could add.

## Common causes

**Token expired.** The channel shows as needing reconnection. Reconnect, then
reschedule — reconnecting does not retry the failed post.

**Rejected content.** The platform refused the media or the text: wrong aspect
ratio, unsupported codec, a link the platform blocks.

**Rate limited.** Platforms cap how much an app can post. Space posts out rather
than scheduling many to one channel at once.

**Duplicate content.** Several platforms silently reject text identical to
something posted recently. Vary the wording.

## One channel failed, the others worked

Expected. Channels publish independently — a failure on one does not hold back
the group.

## Nothing published at all

Check the post is in **queue** and not **draft**. Drafts never publish, and
because they skip validation they are easy to leave incomplete by accident.
