## 1 · What we collect

**Account data:** name, email, phone if you give it, business name, and billing details. Payments are handled by a licensed payment provider; we never store your card number.

**Work content:** posts and drafts, uploaded assets, your publishing plan, team comments, and the tone and red-line lists you define.

**Channel data:** identifiers of connected accounts, and the post metrics each platform allows us to read (reach, engagement, views).

**Usage data:** sign-in records, IP address, device and browser type, and the pages you visit inside the app — for security and product improvement.

## 2 · Channel tokens

When you connect a channel we store an access token, encrypted. We do not read your private messages or DMs, and we use the token only for what you asked for: publishing, scheduling, and reading metrics for your own posts.

Revoking access — in our channel settings or on the social platform — invalidates the token immediately and removes it from our systems.

## 3 · Why we use it

To run the service you asked for (performance of a contract), to protect the platform and prevent abuse (legitimate interest), to meet accounting and tax obligations, and to contact you about your account. Marketing email is sent only with your consent and can be turned off in one click.

## 4 · No training on your content

We do not use your content or your audience data to train AI models. When you generate text, an image or a video, the request is sent to a model provider to perform that one operation, under agreements that prohibit training on it. We do not sell your data to anyone.

## 5 · Service providers

We rely on providers to operate the platform: hosting and databases, file storage, AI model providers for generation, a payment provider, transactional email, and product analytics. Each processes data on our instructions and within a defined scope.

A current list of providers is available on request at privacy@nushir.com, and Enterprise customers are notified before any material addition.

## 6 · Retention and deletion

We keep your workspace content while the account is open. After closure it stays exportable for thirty days, then it is removed from live systems, with backups cleared on their next cycle (90 days maximum). Invoice records are kept for ten years as Saudi regulations require.

Deleting a post or asset in Nushir does not remove what has already been published on a social platform; that has to be done there.

## 7 · Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law you have the right to know how your data is processed, to access it, to request a copy, to correct it, to have it deleted, and to withdraw consent at any time.

Send requests to privacy@nushir.com and we respond within thirty days. If our answer doesn't satisfy you, you may complain to the competent data protection authority in the Kingdom.

## 8 · Security and transfers

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest, and internal access is limited to need and logged. Enterprise customers get single sign-on, a full audit log, and the option of in-Kingdom hosting.

Some operations — generation or email delivery — may be processed by providers outside the Kingdom; where that happens we contract for safeguards that preserve the same level of protection. If a breach affects your data, we notify you and the competent authority as the law requires.
