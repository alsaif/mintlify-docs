## What cookies are

Small files your browser stores when you visit the site. They remember your session and language, and they measure how pages perform. Some are required for sign-in to work; the rest are optional and under your control.

### Your control

Refuse the optional categories in the preferences panel, or clear cookies in your browser settings. Refusing the essential ones means sign-in will not work.

We set no marketing cookies before you consent, and we don't use cookies to profile your audience.
