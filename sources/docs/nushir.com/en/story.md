The name comes from an old Arabic verb: _nashara_ — to unfold something and spread it until it reaches people. That is the whole product: your words arriving, instead of sitting in a draft.

## Why we built Nushir

Small teams in the region are not short on ideas. They are short on time. One founder writes, designs, posts and answers comments — and two weeks later the account goes quiet for a month.

The existing tools were built for the English-speaking market. They schedule, but they don't write in your voice, and they don't know that Friday here isn't Sunday, or that Ramadan is a season of its own.

Nushir starts from the other end: agents that do the daily work, a platform that understands the Arabic context, and one calendar where the decision stays with you.

Arabic first

Not a translated English interface. The copy, the calendar, the analytics and the tone are written for Arabic from the start.

You decide

The agent prepares, you approve. We don't publish in your name without permission, and we don't hide what the agent did.

Your content is yours

We don't train models on your content, and you can revoke any permission or delete your workspace outright.
