## Billing questions

Anything else: [billing@nushir.com](mailto:billing@nushir.com)

How is a channel counted?

Each connected account is one channel, so three Instagram pages count as three. Connect and disconnect freely within your plan's limit.

What if I run out of image or video credits?

Scheduling and publishing continue as normal. Generation pauses until you buy a pack or the new month starts — never an automatic charge.

Can I change plans?

Any time. Upgrades are prorated from the same day, downgrades take effect next cycle, and no content is deleted.

Payment methods?

Mada, Visa, Mastercard and Apple Pay. Enterprise: tax invoice and bank transfer on an annual contract.

Does the trial need a card?

No. Seven days with the full Studio feature set on one channel, no card. Then pick a plan or walk away.
