## What the agent does on Kick

01

### Sets the cadence

It knows how much Kick can carry in a week and spreads your topics without flooding followers.

02

### Writes to the limits

One idea, reshaped for Kick at its own length and register — Short title — in your voice, Arabic or English.

03

### Prepares the media

Stream clips, on your brand, from your own library first and generation second.

04

### Reads the result

Kick numbers feed next month's plan: better times, and topics worth repeating.

01

03

Revoke Kick access at any moment, from our channel settings or from the platform itself.

Quick answers

Does it post without my approval?

No. Drafts land in the calendar by default; you can grant Kick auto-publish on its own if you want.

How much of my plan does it use?

Each connected Kick account counts as one channel. [See the plans](https://nushir.com/en/pricing.html).

Can I post from my own agent?

Yes, over the [API or MCP server](https://nushir.com/en/channels.html#api).
