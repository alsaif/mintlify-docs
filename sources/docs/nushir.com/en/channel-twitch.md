01

### Sets the cadence

It knows how much Twitch can carry in a week and spreads your topics without flooding followers.

02

One idea, reshaped for Twitch at its own length and register — 140 character title — in your voice, Arabic or English.

03

04

### Reads the result

Twitch numbers feed next month's plan: better times, and topics worth repeating.
