01

One idea, reshaped for Discord at its own length and register — 2,000 characters — in your voice, Arabic or English.

Quick answers

Does it post without my approval?

No. Drafts land in the calendar by default; you can grant Discord auto-publish on its own if you want.

How much of my plan does it use?

Each connected Discord account counts as one channel. [See the plans](https://nushir.com/en/pricing.html).

Can I post from my own agent?

Yes, over the [API or MCP server](https://nushir.com/en/channels.html#api).
