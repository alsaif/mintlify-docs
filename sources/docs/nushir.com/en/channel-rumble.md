## What the agent does on Rumble

01

### Sets the cadence

It knows how much Rumble can carry in a week and spreads your topics without flooding followers.

02

### Writes to the limits

One idea, reshaped for Rumble at its own length and register — Title and description — in your voice, Arabic or English.

03

### Prepares the media

Horizontal video, on your brand, from your own library first and generation second.

04

### Reads the result

Rumble numbers feed next month's plan: better times, and topics worth repeating.

Quick answers

Does it post without my approval?

No. Drafts land in the calendar by default; you can grant Rumble auto-publish on its own if you want.

How much of my plan does it use?

Each connected Rumble account counts as one channel. [See the plans](https://nushir.com/en/pricing.html).

Can I post from my own agent?

Yes, over the [API or MCP server](https://nushir.com/en/channels.html#api).
