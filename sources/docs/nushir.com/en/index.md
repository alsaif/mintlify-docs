## Frequently asked

Not answered here? Write to [hey@nushir.com](https://nushir.com/en/pricing.html)

Does the agent publish without my approval?

Only if you let it. By default everything lands as a draft in your calendar; you can grant auto-publish per channel.

How good is the Arabic?

Arabic is the first language of the product, not a translation. Choose Modern Standard, Gulf dialect, or a mix per channel — and the same tone carries into English.

How many channels can I connect?

30+ platforms are supported and each connected account counts as one channel. Your allowance depends on the plan, and channels can be swapped at any time.

Can I run it from my own tools?

Yes. A public REST API, webhooks, an MCP server and a CLI mean your existing agent or automation platform can drive Nushir.

What happens to my content and data?

Your content stays yours. We don't use it to train models, and you can revoke any channel or delete your workspace at any moment.
