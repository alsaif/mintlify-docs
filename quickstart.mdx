This guide walks you through everything you need to get from a blank account to a fully planned, designed, and approved draft ready for publishing. By the end, you will have at least one channel connected, your brand voice saved, and a post sitting in your calendar waiting for your approval.

<Note>
  Auto-publish is **off by default** on every channel. Every post Nushir creates lands as a draft in your calendar. You approve it before it goes anywhere. You can enable auto-publish per channel later in **Settings → Channels**.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [nushir.com](https://platform.nushir.com/auth) and sign up. Your 7-day free trial starts immediately — no credit card required. The trial gives you the full Studio feature set on one channel so you can evaluate everything before choosing a plan.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect a channel">
    Navigate to **Settings → Channels → Add Channel**. Pick the platform you want to start with, then authenticate using OAuth or an API key depending on the platform. Once connected, the account appears in your channel list and is ready for scheduling.

    Each connected account uses one channel slot from your plan's allowance. If you connect three Instagram pages, that counts as three channels. You can disconnect and reconnect channels freely within your plan limit at any time.

    <Tip>
      Start with just one channel. Get comfortable with the workflow — how the Planner fills the calendar, how the Writer produces copy, how approval works — before connecting the rest. Adding more channels later takes under a minute.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your brand">
    Go to **Settings → Brand** and complete your brand profile. This is a one-time setup that all four agents reference on every post they create from that point on.

    Fill in the following:

    - **Tone of voice** — upload 5–10 past posts or write a short description of how your brand sounds. Choose your language register: Modern Standard Arabic, Gulf dialect, English, or a combination. Set your formality level.
    - **Prohibited words** — list any words, phrases, or marketing claims the agents must never use.
    - **Brand assets** — upload your logo and set your brand colors and fonts. The Designer agent applies these automatically to every image and video it creates.
    - **Media library** — upload your own photos, videos, and design assets. The Designer always reaches for your real materials first before generating anything new.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Let the Planner build your month">
    Navigate to **Calendar → New Plan**. Enter your goal for the month in plain language — for example, "Launch product v2 on September 1 with a build-up campaign across Instagram and LinkedIn." Optionally mark any key dates you want the plan to anchor around. Click **Generate Plan**.

    The Planner builds a draft calendar and opens it in Calendar view. You will see proposed post slots distributed across your connected channels at a realistic posting cadence, with topic suggestions for each slot.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and approve drafts">
    Open any post slot in the Calendar. The Writer has already filled most slots with copy, and the Designer has attached media sized correctly for that platform. Read the draft, make any edits you want directly in the editor, then click **Approve**.

    Any edits you make are remembered by the Writer as tone feedback — it uses them to improve future drafts automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go live">
    Approved posts publish automatically at their scheduled time. You do not need to do anything else. Check back in **Analytics** after 7 days — the Analyst agent will have a written summary of what performed well, the best times to post for your audience, and recommendations for the next cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>
