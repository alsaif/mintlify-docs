LinkedIn rewards depth and consistency — thought-leadership posts, carousel documents, and long-form articles perform best when they show up regularly and stay on brand. That level of output is hard to sustain manually across a company page and a personal profile. Nushir's AI agent handles the planning, writing, and media preparation so you stay present on LinkedIn without it consuming your week. Every draft lands in your calendar; approve, edit, or reschedule before anything publishes.

## Platform Specs

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Posts, Articles, Images, Documents (PDF) |
| **Text limit** | 3,000 characters |
| **Media** | PDF uploaded as a native slideshow |
| **Account types** | Company page or personal account |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plans your posting rhythm">
    The agent sets a sustainable LinkedIn cadence — typically three to five posts per week for most accounts — and balances content types across the month: text posts, image posts, document carousels, and article links. It keeps your topic mix varied so your audience sees different angles of your expertise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writes within 3,000 characters in your tone">
    LinkedIn readers expect professional, narrative-driven content. The agent writes each post within the 3,000-character limit in a tone that matches your brand — whether that's authoritative, conversational, or technical — in Arabic or English. Your content restrictions and banned phrases are applied automatically to every draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepares PDFs and images">
    For document posts, the agent assembles a PDF from your brand assets and uploads it as a native LinkedIn slideshow — the format that consistently earns the most dwell time on the platform. For image posts, it pulls from your media library or generates on-brand visuals when needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reads results to sharpen the next plan">
    After publishing, the agent reads your LinkedIn analytics — impressions, reactions, comments, shares, follower growth — and uses those signals to improve the next month's content mix, posting times, and topic priorities.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect LinkedIn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select **LinkedIn**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate with LinkedIn">
    You'll be redirected to LinkedIn's OAuth flow. Sign in and choose whether you're connecting a personal account or a company page. Review the permissions, then click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your tone and restrictions">
    Choose your language, describe your professional voice, and list any topics or phrases the agent should avoid — for example, competitor names or sensitive company matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review drafts in your calendar">
    Drafts appear in the content calendar. Click any post to edit the text, update the PDF, or shift the publish time. Approve when you're satisfied — the post goes out at the scheduled time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can disconnect LinkedIn at any time from **Channel Settings** in Nushir, or by revoking access from LinkedIn's own security settings page.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the agent post to LinkedIn without my approval?">
    No. Drafts default to **Pending** in your calendar. Auto-publish is an opt-in setting available per channel — it is not enabled unless you turn it on explicitly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does each LinkedIn account count as one channel slot?">
    Yes. A personal account and a company page are each counted as a separate channel slot. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) to check how many channels your plan allows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish to LinkedIn via the API or MCP?">
    Yes. REST API and MCP publishing are fully supported for LinkedIn, just like every other Nushir channel. See the [API & MCP documentation](/channels/overview#custom-channel) for setup instructions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
