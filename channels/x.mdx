X moves fast — a post that would land perfectly on Monday morning is stale by afternoon. Nushir's AI agent keeps you in the conversation without requiring you to monitor the clock. Connect your X account once, define your voice, and the agent plans a posting rhythm, writes within the 280-character limit, lines up your media, and queues every draft for your review. For multi-post Threads, the agent writes and sequences each post in order so the narrative flows when it publishes.

## Platform Specs

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Posts, Threads, Images, Video |
| **Text limit** | 280 characters per post |
| **Media** | Up to 4 images or 1 video |
| **Threads** | Published in sequential order automatically |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plans your posting rhythm">
    The agent figures out how many posts per week keep you visible on X without flooding your followers' timelines. It spaces topics so your feed covers your priorities without repeating itself, and it reserves slots for timely content you add manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writes within 280 characters">
    X rewards brevity and directness. The agent rewrites every piece of content to fit 280 characters per post — punchy, clear, and in your tone — in Arabic or English. For Threads, it breaks a longer idea into a sequenced series of posts, each one standing on its own while building toward the conclusion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepares images or video">
    The agent attaches up to 4 images or 1 video to each post, pulling from your media library first. If nothing suitable is available, it generates on-brand images. All media is formatted to X's aspect ratios before it reaches your draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reads results to refine the next plan">
    After each post, the agent collects impressions, link clicks, reposts, and engagement rate from X. Those numbers feed directly into the next month's plan — better times, better topics, better formats.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect X

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select **X**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate with X">
    You'll be redirected to X's OAuth flow. Sign in and review the permissions — Nushir needs read and write access to post on your behalf — then click **Authorize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your tone and restrictions">
    Choose your language, describe your brand voice, and list any topics or phrases the agent should never use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review drafts in your calendar">
    Your first drafts appear in the content calendar. Edit any post inline, adjust the schedule by dragging it to a new time slot, and click **Approve** when you're ready. Threads appear as grouped posts you can expand and reorder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can revoke X's access at any time from **Channel Settings** in Nushir, or from the connected apps section of your X account settings.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. All drafts default to **Pending** in your calendar. Auto-publish is an opt-in option you can enable for X specifically — it is never switched on without your action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does each X account count as one channel slot?">
    Yes. Each connected X account uses one channel slot from your plan. If you manage multiple X accounts, each one is counted separately. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for plan limits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish to X via the API or MCP?">
    Yes. REST API and MCP publishing are supported on all Nushir channels, including X. You can trigger posts from external tools, automation workflows, or your own AI agents. See the [API & MCP documentation](/channels/overview#custom-channel) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
