WhatsApp Business gives you a direct line to your customers, and Nushir keeps it active without the daily manual effort. Connect your Meta-verified account once, and the agent drafts status updates and list messages that fit inside your approved templates, respects the 1,024-character limit, and waits for your review before anything is sent.

<Note>
  WhatsApp Business requires a **Meta-approved Business account**. If you haven't been approved yet, start the verification process in Meta Business Manager. Template approval can take **24–48 hours**, so plan your first batch of content accordingly.
</Note>

## Channel Specs

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Status updates, list messages |
| **Text limit** | 1,024 characters |
| **Media** | Image or video |
| **Platform features** | Approved templates required |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plans your rhythm">
    The agent determines an appropriate cadence for WhatsApp Business — a channel where over-posting can hurt engagement — and spaces your messages so your audience stays receptive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writes within platform limits">
    Your message is crafted to fit WhatsApp's 1,024-character limit and structured to work inside your pre-approved templates, in your brand voice and your preferred language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepares media">
    Images and videos are selected or generated on-brand, pulled from your media library first, with AI generation as a fallback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reads the results">
    Delivery and engagement data from WhatsApp Business flow back into the next month's plan, surfacing the best send windows and highest-performing message types.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the channel">
    Go to **Settings → Channels → Add Channel** and select **WhatsApp Business**. You'll review the permissions before approving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate via Meta">
    Complete the Meta Business OAuth flow. Nushir connects to your WhatsApp Business account using only the permissions you grant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your tone and restrictions">
    Define your brand voice, topics to avoid, and how frequently the agent should draft messages for this channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review drafts in the calendar">
    All drafts land in your content calendar. Approve, edit, or reschedule before they go out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep your approved template library up to date in Meta Business Manager. The more templates Nushir can choose from, the more variety your scheduled messages will have.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will Nushir publish without my approval?">
    No. Every message starts as a draft in your calendar. You can opt in to auto-publish for WhatsApp Business in the channel settings if you want fully automated sending.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my templates haven't been approved yet?">
    The agent will draft the content and queue it, but it won't publish until a matching approved template exists. Template approval typically takes 24–48 hours through Meta Business Manager.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does this count against my plan?">
    Each connected WhatsApp Business account uses one channel slot. Check the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for the number of slots available on your plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish via API or MCP?">
    Yes. You can trigger WhatsApp Business messages programmatically through the Nushir REST API or via MCP from your own agent or automation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I revoke access?">
    Yes — at any time from Nushir's channel settings, or directly through Meta Business Manager.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
