Kick is one of the fastest-growing live streaming platforms, and creators who build an audience there know that consistent clip sharing and pre-scheduled announcements are what keep followers coming back between streams. Nushir's AI agent connects to your Kick account once, drafts concise titles and descriptions that fit the platform's style, and queues stream clips for pre-scheduled publishing — pausing for your approval before anything goes out.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Clips + Stream announcements |
| **Text limit** | Short, concise title |
| **Media** | Stream clips from your channel |
| **Platform note** | Supports pre-scheduling of posts |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent builds a posting schedule around your streaming calendar, identifying the best moments to share clips and announcements so your Kick channel stays active and visible even on non-stream days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Titles and announcement copy are kept concise and punchy, matching Kick's platform style while reflecting your brand's voice — whether you're teasing a highlight clip or promoting an upcoming stream.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    The agent selects clips from your stream archive, trims and packages them for posting, and pairs them with the written content — ready to go at your pre-scheduled time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    Engagement data from your Kick posts flows back into the planning loop, helping the agent refine timing and content selection for the next scheduling cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select Kick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    You'll be redirected to Kick's authorization flow. Review the permissions and approve to connect your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Configure your brand voice, preferred content style, and any topics to avoid — applied to every draft the agent creates for Kick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Pre-scheduled clips and announcements appear in your content calendar. Approve or adjust before they publish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can revoke Kick access at any time from your Nushir channel settings or from your Kick account's connected apps section.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. All content is queued as a draft by default. You approve each post before it goes live. You can opt in to auto-publish for Kick individually in your channel settings if you want fully automated pre-scheduled posting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting Kick use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected Kick account counts as one channel slot in your plan. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits included in each plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish Kick content via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint support Kick publishing, letting you trigger clip posts and announcements programmatically. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
