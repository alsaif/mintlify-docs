Pinterest is a discovery engine as much as a social platform, and creators who pin consistently with strong descriptions and organised boards build compounding traffic over time. Nushir's AI agent connects to your Pinterest account once, then handles the scheduling workflow: planning a sustainable pinning cadence across your boards, writing 500-character descriptions optimised for Pinterest search, sourcing vertical 2:3 images, and attaching destination links — all drafted for your approval before anything is posted.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Pins + Boards |
| **Text limit** | 500 characters for the description |
| **Media** | Vertical 2:3 aspect ratio images |
| **Platform note** | One destination link per Pin |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent maps your content themes to specific Pinterest boards and builds a pinning schedule around peak discovery times, ensuring a steady flow of new pins that keeps your boards active and surfaced by Pinterest's recommendation algorithm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Each Pin description is written within Pinterest's 500-character limit, using natural keyword language that matches how people search for and save content on the platform — all in your brand voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    Vertical 2:3 images are sourced from your media library and formatted to Pinterest's preferred aspect ratio. The agent attaches the relevant destination link to each Pin and assigns it to the correct board.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    Impression, save, and outbound click data from Pinterest flows back into the planning cycle, helping the agent identify which boards and content types drive the most traffic and saves over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select Pinterest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    You'll be redirected to Pinterest's OAuth screen. Review the permissions and approve to connect your account and boards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Configure your brand voice, board preferences, and link strategy once — applied to every Pin draft the agent creates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Scheduled Pins appear in your content calendar. Review the image, description, link, and board assignment before approving each one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can revoke Pinterest access at any time from your Nushir channel settings or from Pinterest's connected apps page.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. Every Pin is queued as a draft by default. You review and approve each one before it's posted to your boards. You can enable auto-publish for Pinterest individually in your channel settings if you prefer a fully automated pinning flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting Pinterest use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected Pinterest account counts as one channel slot in your plan. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish Pinterest Pins via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint support Pinterest publishing, so you can create and schedule Pins programmatically from your own tools. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
