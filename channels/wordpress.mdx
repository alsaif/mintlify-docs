WordPress powers more than 40% of the web, and a well-maintained blog is one of the highest-ROI content channels you can run — but producing well-structured articles week after week is time-consuming. Nushir's AI agent connects to your WordPress site once and handles the full drafting pipeline: planning your editorial calendar, writing long-form posts with no character limit, sourcing featured images, and assigning categories and tags. By default, every post lands in WordPress as a draft, giving you full editorial control before anything goes live; you can switch to auto-publish any time you're ready to hand off that step.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Articles + Drafts |
| **Text limit** | No text limit |
| **Media** | Featured image per post |
| **Platform note** | Categories and tags supported |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent builds an editorial calendar for your WordPress blog — balancing topic clusters, SEO opportunities, and publishing frequency so your site stays fresh without stretching your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Long-form articles are written without any length constraint, structured with headings, subheadings, and natural keyword placement to suit both readers and search engines — all in your brand voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    Featured images are sourced from your media library or generated by the agent when originals aren't available. Categories and tags are assigned automatically based on the article's topic and your existing taxonomy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    Page views, time-on-page, and other WordPress analytics feed back into the agent's planning loop, revealing which topics and formats drive the most traffic and engagement over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select WordPress.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Connect via your WordPress.com account or by providing REST API credentials for a self-hosted site. Nushir only requests the permissions it needs to create and manage posts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Define your editorial voice, topic focus, and any content restrictions once. The agent applies these to every article it drafts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Drafted articles appear in your Nushir calendar and as WordPress drafts in your dashboard. Review, edit, and publish when you're satisfied.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Posts are created as WordPress **drafts** by default — nothing is published until you explicitly approve it. You can enable auto-publish in your channel settings if you'd like the agent to publish directly without a manual review step.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. Every article is created as a WordPress draft by default and waits for your approval in both Nushir's calendar and your WordPress admin. If you'd prefer fully automated publishing, you can opt in to auto-publish for this channel in your settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting WordPress use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected WordPress site counts as one channel slot in your plan. If you run multiple sites, each one occupies its own slot. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish WordPress posts via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint support WordPress publishing, so you can create and publish posts programmatically from your own tools or pipelines. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
