TikTok rewards consistency, but keeping up a daily posting rhythm while running a business is nearly impossible on your own. Nushir's AI agent connects to your TikTok account once, then works inside your content calendar to plan a sustainable cadence, write descriptions in your voice, and queue vertical video and photo content — pausing at every draft so you stay in control of what your audience sees.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Short video + Photos |
| **Text limit** | 2,200 characters for the description |
| **Media** | Video up to 10 minutes |
| **Platform note** | Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent calculates a sustainable TikTok posting cadence for your account — spacing topics across the week so your feed stays active without overwhelming your followers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Every description is crafted specifically for TikTok's 2,200-character limit, matching your brand tone whether you post in English or any other language you've configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    The agent sources vertical 9:16 video (up to 10 minutes) and photo assets from your media library first, falling back to AI generation when original material isn't available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    After a post goes live, TikTok performance data feeds back into next month's plan — surfacing better posting times and topics worth repeating.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select TikTok.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    You'll be redirected to TikTok's OAuth screen. Review the requested permissions and approve — Nushir only asks for what it needs to schedule and publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Define your brand voice, any content restrictions, and preferred posting times once. The agent applies these settings to every draft it creates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Your TikTok drafts appear in the calendar, ready for your review and approval before anything is published.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can revoke TikTok access at any time from your Nushir channel settings or directly from TikTok's connected apps page.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. Every post lands in your calendar as a draft by default. You review and approve each one before it goes live. If you'd prefer hands-free operation, you can opt in to auto-publish for TikTok individually in your channel settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting TikTok use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected TikTok account counts as one channel slot in your plan. If you manage multiple TikTok accounts, each one occupies its own slot. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish TikTok posts via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir exposes a publishing API and an MCP endpoint, so you can trigger TikTok posts programmatically from your own tools or automations. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
