YouTube is the world's second-largest search engine, and every video you publish is a long-term asset — but writing compelling titles, crafting SEO-ready descriptions, and keeping a consistent upload schedule takes serious time. Nushir's AI agent connects to your YouTube channel once, then handles the planning and writing work so you can focus on creating great video content, stepping in only when you need to approve a draft.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Long-form video + Shorts |
| **Text limit** | 100 characters for the title; 5,000 characters for the description |
| **Media** | Custom thumbnail supported |
| **Platform note** | Supports both standard 16:9 videos and vertical Shorts |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent determines a healthy upload cadence for your channel, distributing topics across the week and month so your subscribers stay engaged without triggering burnout on your end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Titles are crafted to land within YouTube's 100-character limit while maximising click-through potential. Descriptions are written up to 5,000 characters, structured with keywords, timestamps, and calls to action — all in your brand voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    The agent prepares custom thumbnail assets from your media library first, using AI generation as a fallback. For Shorts, it formats content to the vertical 9:16 specification automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    Post-publish analytics from YouTube feed back into the next content plan, highlighting which topics drove watch time, which upload times performed best, and what's worth repeating.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select YouTube.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    You'll be taken to Google's OAuth screen. Review the permissions and approve — Nushir requests only what's needed to schedule and publish to your channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Configure your brand voice, topic preferences, and any content restrictions once. The agent applies these to every draft title and description it writes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Your YouTube drafts appear in the content calendar. Approve, edit, or reschedule each one before it publishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can disconnect your YouTube channel at any time from Nushir's channel settings or from your Google account's connected apps page.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. All posts are saved as drafts in your calendar by default. You approve each one before it goes live. If you want to skip the review step, you can enable auto-publish for YouTube on its own in your channel settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting YouTube use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected YouTube channel counts as one channel slot in your plan. Managing a second channel requires a second slot. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish YouTube videos via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint both support YouTube publishing, so you can trigger uploads programmatically from your own workflows. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
