Growing a Twitch community means more than going live — it means consistently surfacing your best clips, teasing upcoming streams, and keeping followers in the loop between broadcasts. Nushir's AI agent connects to your Twitch account once and handles the scheduling work automatically: drafting titles within the 140-character limit, cueing clips from your stream archive, and preparing follower notifications — all held in draft until you give the go-ahead.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Clips + Stream announcements |
| **Text limit** | 140 characters for the title |
| **Media** | Clips sourced from your own stream |
| **Platform note** | Follower notifications supported |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent reviews your streaming schedule and builds a clip-and-announcement cadence around it, so you're engaging followers on days you're not live as well as on broadcast days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Clip titles and stream announcements are written to fit Twitch's 140-character title limit — punchy, on-brand, and crafted to drive clicks from your follower feed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    The agent pulls clips directly from your Twitch stream archive, selects highlights based on engagement signals, and packages them for posting with the relevant title and description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    View counts, clip shares, and follower notification open rates flow back into the agent's planning loop, sharpening future scheduling around what your audience responds to most.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select Twitch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    You'll be redirected to Twitch's OAuth screen. Review the permissions and approve to link your channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Set your brand voice, preferred content types, and any restrictions once. These apply to every draft the agent creates for your Twitch channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Scheduled clips and announcements appear in your calendar. Approve or edit each draft before it publishes and notifies your followers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can disconnect your Twitch account at any time from Nushir's channel settings or from Twitch's own connections page.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. Every clip post and announcement is created as a draft by default. You review and approve before anything reaches your followers. You can enable auto-publish for Twitch individually in your channel settings if you prefer a hands-off flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting Twitch use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected Twitch account counts as one channel slot in your plan. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish Twitch content via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint support Twitch publishing, so you can trigger clip posts and announcements programmatically from your own tools. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
