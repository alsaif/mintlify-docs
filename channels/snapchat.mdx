Snapchat's Stories and Spotlight formats reward brands that show up daily with fresh, vertical content — but building that output manually, every single day, drains your team fast. Nushir's AI agent connects to your Snapchat account once, then takes care of planning a sustainable cadence, scripting content to fit the platform's 60-second story format, and preparing vertical 9:16 assets — only moving forward once you've reviewed and approved each draft.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Stories + Spotlight |
| **Text limit** | Stories up to 60 seconds |
| **Media** | Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio |
| **Platform note** | Swipe-up link supported per Story |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent maps out a daily or weekly Snapchat posting schedule suited to your audience, balancing Story sequences with Spotlight submissions so you're always present without burning out your content pipeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Scripts and overlays are shaped to work within the 60-second Story format, with concise, on-brand text designed for quick vertical viewing — in your preferred language and tone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    Assets are pulled from your media library and formatted to the vertical 9:16 spec. The agent adds swipe-up links where relevant and falls back to AI-generated visuals when source material isn't available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    Snapchat reach and engagement data flows back after each post, informing the next round of scheduling with better timing and higher-performing content angles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select Snapchat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    You'll be redirected to Snapchat's OAuth flow. Review the permissions requested and approve to link your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Define your brand voice and any off-limits topics once. These settings apply to every Snapchat draft the agent creates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Drafts land in your content calendar. Review, adjust, or approve each Story or Spotlight before it publishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can revoke Snapchat access at any time from your Nushir channel settings or directly from your Snapchat account's app permissions page.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. The default mode is draft — every Story and Spotlight waits in your calendar for your sign-off. You can opt in to auto-publish for Snapchat specifically if you want a fully automated flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting Snapchat use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected Snapchat account counts as one channel slot. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits included in each plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish Snapchat content via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint support Snapchat publishing, letting you trigger posts programmatically from external tools or automations. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
