Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing a potential customer sees, and keeping it stocked with fresh updates, active offers, and upcoming events signals that your business is alive and engaged. Connect your profile once, and Nushir's agent drafts updates, offers, and events within the 1,500-character limit, prepares a cover image for each post, and adds call-to-action buttons — then waits in your calendar for approval before publishing.

## Channel Specs

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Updates, offers, events |
| **Text limit** | 1,500 characters |
| **Media** | One image per update |
| **Platform features** | Call-to-action button |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plans your rhythm">
    The agent maps out a posting cadence that keeps your Google Business Profile consistently active — mixing regular updates with timely offers and upcoming events to give searchers a complete picture of your business.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writes within platform limits">
    Each post is written to fit within Google's 1,500-character limit, with the appropriate post type selected (update, offer, or event) and the right call-to-action button configured for your goal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepares media">
    A cover image is selected or generated for each post, sourced from your media library first and created with AI when needed, sized and formatted to Google's specifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reads the results">
    Views, clicks, and call-to-action engagement data from your Business Profile feed back into the next planning cycle, helping the agent identify which post types and topics drive the most customer actions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the channel">
    Go to **Settings → Channels → Add Channel** and select **Google Business**. You'll see exactly what permissions are requested before you approve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate with Google">
    Complete the Google OAuth flow and select the Business Profile you want to connect. If you manage multiple locations, you can add each one as a separate channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your tone and restrictions">
    Configure the brand voice, any topics to avoid, and whether the agent should default to updates, offers, or a mix of post types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review drafts in the calendar">
    Drafts appear in your content calendar with the post type, image, and CTA button shown. Approve, edit, or reschedule each one before it publishes to your profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Offers and events need start and end dates. When you brief the agent on an upcoming promotion or event, include the date range so it can schedule the post and automatically set the right expiry.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will Nushir publish without my approval?">
    No. Every post starts as a draft in your calendar. You can opt in to auto-publish from the channel settings if you want a fully automated flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I manage multiple business locations?">
    Yes. Each Google Business Profile location is added as a separate channel slot. The agent manages each location's content independently with its own tone and posting cadence.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which call-to-action buttons are supported?">
    The agent supports all standard Google Business CTA types: **Book**, **Order online**, **Buy**, **Learn more**, **Sign up**, and **Call**. You configure the default CTA in the channel tone settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does this count against my plan?">
    Each connected Google Business Profile uses one channel slot. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for how many slots your plan includes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish via API or MCP?">
    Yes. You can post to your Google Business Profile programmatically using the Nushir REST API or via MCP.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
