Email remains one of the highest-converting channels you can invest in, but writing and scheduling a consistent newsletter on top of everything else you're managing is a real drain. Nushir's AI agent connects to your Mailchimp account once, then handles the content side of the workflow: planning a sending cadence that fits your audience expectations, writing full-length newsletter copy in your voice, applying your branded templates, and targeting the right list segments — all drafted for your review before a single email is scheduled in Mailchimp.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Emails + Newsletters |
| **Text limit** | No text limit |
| **Media** | Branded templates from your Mailchimp account |
| **Platform note** | List segmentation supported |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent builds an email sending calendar around your audience's engagement patterns — recommending send frequency, optimal send times, and a topic mix that keeps your list engaged and your unsubscribe rate low.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Newsletter copy is written without any length constraint, structured as clearly sectioned emails that work inside Mailchimp's template system — subject lines, preview text, body content, and calls to action all written in your brand voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    The agent applies your existing branded Mailchimp templates to each newsletter, inserting the written content into the right blocks. List segmentation is configured per campaign so each send reaches the most relevant subscribers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    Open rates, click rates, and unsubscribe data from Mailchimp flow back into the planning cycle, letting the agent refine send timing, subject line style, and content topics based on what your list actually responds to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select Mailchimp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    You'll be redirected to Mailchimp's OAuth screen. Review the permissions and approve to link your account, audiences, and templates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Configure your email voice, preferred sending frequency, default audience segments, and any topics to avoid — applied to every newsletter draft the agent creates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Scheduled newsletters appear in your Nushir calendar. Review the subject line, preview text, body copy, template, and segment before approving each campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can revoke Mailchimp access at any time from your Nushir channel settings or from your Mailchimp account's connected apps page.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent send emails without my approval?">
    No. Every newsletter is created as a draft and staged in your Nushir calendar first — nothing is scheduled in Mailchimp until you review and approve it. You can enable auto-publish for Mailchimp individually in your channel settings if you'd prefer a fully automated sending flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting Mailchimp use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected Mailchimp account counts as one channel slot in your plan. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I schedule Mailchimp campaigns via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint support Mailchimp campaign creation and scheduling, so you can trigger newsletter sends programmatically from your own tools or automations. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
