Nushir treats Telegram as a first-class publishing destination. Connect your channel once, and the AI agent takes care of the entire content cycle — from planning how often to post, to writing within Telegram's 4,096-character limit, to preparing albums and file attachments — then stops and waits for your approval before anything goes live.

## Channel Specs

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Channel messages, images, files |
| **Text limit** | 4,096 characters |
| **Media** | Albums and files |
| **Platform features** | Inline buttons and links |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plans your rhythm">
    The agent knows how many posts a Telegram channel can comfortably absorb each week and distributes your topics so your subscribers never feel overwhelmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writes within platform limits">
    Your idea is shaped specifically for Telegram — up to 4,096 characters — in your brand voice, in English or Arabic, at whatever tone you've set for this channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepares media">
    Albums and file attachments are assembled on-brand, drawing from your media library first and falling back to AI generation when needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reads the results">
    After publishing, Telegram analytics feed back into next month's plan: better send times and topics worth repeating.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the channel">
    In Nushir, go to **Settings → Channels → Add Channel** and select **Telegram**. You'll be shown the exact permissions requested before you approve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Complete the Telegram OAuth flow. Nushir receives only the publishing permissions you explicitly grant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your tone and restrictions">
    Tell the agent your brand voice, any topics to avoid, and your preferred posting frequency for this channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review drafts in the calendar">
    Drafts appear in your content calendar. Approve, edit, or reschedule each one before it publishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can revoke Nushir's access to your Telegram channel at any time — either from Nushir's channel settings or directly from within Telegram itself.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will Nushir publish without my approval?">
    No. The default mode is **draft** — every post sits in your calendar waiting for your sign-off. If you want hands-free publishing for this channel, you can opt in to auto-publish from the channel settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does this count against my plan?">
    Each connected Telegram account uses one channel slot in your plan. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per tier.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish from my own agent or via API?">
    Yes. Nushir supports publishing to Telegram through the REST API and via MCP, so you can trigger posts programmatically or from your own AI agent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the agent understand Telegram-specific formatting?">
    Yes. The Writer agent knows Telegram's markdown and HTML formatting options and applies them to make your messages render correctly in the channel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I schedule posts to multiple Telegram channels?">
    Each connected Telegram channel is a separate channel slot. Add as many as your plan allows, and the agent manages each one's rhythm independently.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
