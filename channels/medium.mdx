Medium's built-in distribution network gives well-written articles a genuine shot at reaching new readers beyond your existing following — but only if you publish consistently and tag your work correctly. Nushir's AI agent connects to your Medium account once, then manages the editorial workflow: planning a sustainable publishing cadence, writing full-length articles and newsletter editions in your voice, sourcing cover images, and selecting up to 5 tags to maximise topic reach — all waiting in draft until you approve.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Articles + Newsletters |
| **Text limit** | No text limit |
| **Media** | Cover image per article |
| **Platform note** | Up to 5 tags per post |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent builds a Medium publishing schedule around your content themes, ensuring a steady stream of articles and newsletter issues that keep your followers engaged and attract new readers through Medium's topic feeds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Articles and newsletter editions are written without any length constraint, crafted to match Medium's thoughtful, essay-style format while staying true to your brand voice. The agent structures each piece for readability and reader retention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    Cover images are sourced from your media library or generated by the agent when originals aren't available. Up to 5 topic tags are selected per post to ensure the right audiences discover your content through Medium's recommendation engine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    Post views, reads, claps, and follower data from Medium flow back into the planning cycle, surfacing which topics and formats resonate most with your audience for future issues.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select Medium.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    You'll be redirected to Medium's OAuth screen. Review the permissions and approve to link your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Configure your writing style, topic focus, and preferred tags once — the agent applies these across every article and newsletter it drafts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Drafted articles appear in your Nushir calendar. Approve each piece before it publishes to your Medium profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can revoke Medium access at any time from your Nushir channel settings or from your Medium account's security settings.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. Every article and newsletter is saved as a draft by default and waits in your calendar for your review. You can enable auto-publish for Medium individually in your channel settings if you prefer a hands-off flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting Medium use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected Medium account counts as one channel slot in your plan. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish Medium articles via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint support Medium publishing, letting you create and publish articles programmatically from your own tools. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
