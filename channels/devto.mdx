Dev.to is one of the most active developer communities on the internet, and creators who publish regularly with well-chosen tags reach thousands of practitioners looking for exactly what they've built or learned. Nushir's AI agent connects to your Dev.to account once, then handles the content work: planning a technical publishing cadence, writing Markdown articles that fit the platform's community tone, building out article series, and selecting up to 4 tags per post — all drafted for your review before going live.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Developer articles |
| **Text limit** | Full Markdown supported |
| **Media** | Article series organisation |
| **Platform note** | Up to 4 tags per article |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent builds a developer content calendar covering tutorials, project walkthroughs, tool comparisons, and opinion pieces — organised into coherent article series so your Dev.to profile tells a consistent story to readers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Articles are written in full Markdown with proper heading structure, inline code, fenced code blocks, and developer-friendly formatting. Each post is crafted to match Dev.to's community-first, practical tone while reflecting your own voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    Code snippets are embedded and formatted for Dev.to's renderer. The agent assigns each article to the correct series and selects up to 4 relevant tags to reach the right developer sub-communities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    Reactions, comments, reading time, and follower growth from Dev.to feed back into the planning cycle, helping the agent refine which topics and formats perform best with the community.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select Dev.to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Enter your Dev.to API key. Nushir uses it solely to create and manage posts on your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Define your technical focus, preferred tags, series structure, and writing style once — applied to every draft the agent creates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Draft articles land in your Nushir calendar. Review the Markdown and metadata, then approve to publish to your Dev.to profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can remove your Dev.to API key and disconnect the channel at any time from your Nushir channel settings.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. Every article is created as a draft by default and waits in your Nushir calendar for your sign-off before it's published to Dev.to. You can enable auto-publish for Dev.to individually in your channel settings if you prefer a fully automated flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting Dev.to use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected Dev.to account counts as one channel slot in your plan. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish Dev.to articles via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint support Dev.to publishing, so you can create and trigger posts programmatically from your own tools or CI pipelines. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
