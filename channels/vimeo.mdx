Vimeo is the platform of choice for creators and teams who prioritise video quality, professional presentation, and fine-grained privacy controls. Nushir's AI agent connects to your Vimeo account once and handles the scheduling layer: crafting titles within the 128-character limit, writing descriptions that complement your footage, and managing privacy settings per video — all queued in draft mode until you're ready to publish.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | High-quality video |
| **Text limit** | 128 characters for the title |
| **Media** | Video up to 4K resolution |
| **Platform note** | Configurable privacy per video (public, private, or password-protected) |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent builds a Vimeo upload schedule that aligns with your project timelines and audience expectations, spacing releases to maintain a steady, professional presence on your Vimeo portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Every video title is written to fit within Vimeo's 128-character limit while being descriptive and search-friendly. Descriptions are written to match the considered, high-quality tone that Vimeo's audience expects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    The agent retrieves your video files from your media library and packages them with the correct metadata — including privacy settings, tags, and description — ready for upload at the scheduled time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    View, play rate, and engagement data from Vimeo feed back into the planning cycle, helping refine future release timing and content framing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select Vimeo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    You'll be taken to Vimeo's OAuth screen. Review the permissions and approve to link your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Define your brand voice, default privacy preference, and any content guidelines once — applied to every draft the agent creates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Scheduled uploads appear in your calendar. Review the title, description, and privacy setting for each, then approve when you're ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can disconnect your Vimeo account at any time from Nushir's channel settings or from Vimeo's own app permissions page.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. Every video upload is staged as a draft by default. You review and approve each one before it goes live on Vimeo. You can enable auto-publish for Vimeo individually in your channel settings if you prefer an automated workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting Vimeo use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected Vimeo account counts as one channel slot in your plan. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish Vimeo videos via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint both support Vimeo publishing, so you can trigger video uploads programmatically from your own workflows. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
