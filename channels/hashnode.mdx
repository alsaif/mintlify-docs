Hashnode is the go-to blogging platform for developers who want to own their audience while still benefiting from a built-in community of technical readers. Nushir's AI agent connects to your Hashnode blog once and takes the repetitive work out of maintaining a consistent publishing cadence: planning your technical content calendar, writing full Markdown articles with properly formatted code snippets, and pushing drafts straight to your custom domain — all staged for your review before they go live.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Technical articles |
| **Text limit** | Full Markdown supported |
| **Media** | Code snippets with syntax highlighting |
| **Platform note** | Published to your own custom domain |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent designs a technical content calendar that covers the topics your developer audience cares about — tutorials, deep dives, tool reviews, and opinion pieces — spaced at a cadence your schedule can support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Articles are written in full Markdown with proper heading hierarchy, inline code, and fenced code blocks with language-specific syntax highlighting. The agent structures each post for skimmability and technical accuracy, in your preferred voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    Code snippets are formatted and embedded directly within the Markdown. Where header images or diagrams are needed, the agent sources them from your media library or generates them as a fallback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    Hashnode reading time, reactions, and comment activity feed back into the planning process — identifying which technical topics drive the most engagement from your developer audience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select Hashnode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Enter your Hashnode API key. Nushir uses it only to create and manage posts on your blog — no broader account access required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Define your writing style, preferred tech stack topics, and code formatting conventions once. The agent applies these to every article it drafts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Technical articles land in your Nushir calendar as drafts. Review the Markdown, inspect the code snippets, and approve when you're happy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can remove your Hashnode API key and disconnect the channel at any time from your Nushir channel settings.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. Every article is staged as a draft by default — nothing is pushed to your Hashnode blog until you've reviewed and approved it in Nushir. You can enable auto-publish for Hashnode in your channel settings if you'd like to skip the manual approval step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting Hashnode use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected Hashnode blog counts as one channel slot in your plan. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish Hashnode articles via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint support Hashnode publishing, so you can create and publish technical posts programmatically from your own pipelines. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
