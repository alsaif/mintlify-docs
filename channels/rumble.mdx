Rumble's growing creator community rewards publishers who upload consistently and organise their content into clear, well-labelled playlists. Nushir's AI agent connects to your Rumble account once, then takes care of the repetitive scheduling work: writing titles and descriptions for each upload, queuing horizontal video files from your media library, and slotting content into the right playlist — all drafted for your review before anything is submitted.

## Platform Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Videos + Clips |
| **Text limit** | Title + description (no fixed character cap enforced by the platform) |
| **Media** | Horizontal video format |
| **Platform note** | Playlist organisation supported |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Rhythm">
    The agent maps out an upload schedule that keeps your Rumble channel active and growing — spacing videos across the week and grouping related content into playlist sequences for better discoverability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Within Limits">
    Titles and descriptions are written to be clear, searchable, and on-brand. The agent adapts the copy to match Rumble's direct, informative content culture while staying true to your voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Media">
    Horizontal video files are pulled from your media library and paired with the written metadata, with playlist assignment handled automatically based on topic and series grouping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Results">
    View counts and engagement data from Rumble flow back into the planning cycle, highlighting which topics and upload times perform best for your audience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select Rumble.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    You'll be redirected to Rumble's authorization page. Review the permissions and approve to connect your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Tone">
    Configure your brand voice, preferred content style, and playlist structure once — the agent applies these settings to every draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Drafts">
    Scheduled uploads appear in your content calendar. Approve or adjust titles, descriptions, and playlist assignments before publishing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can revoke Rumble access at any time from your Nushir channel settings or directly from your Rumble account.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the agent publish without my approval?">
    No. All uploads are staged as drafts by default. You review and approve each video before it's submitted to Rumble. You can opt in to auto-publish for Rumble individually in your channel settings if you prefer an automated flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting Rumble use one of my channel slots?">
    Yes — each connected Rumble account counts as one channel slot in your plan. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for slot limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish Rumble videos via API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir's API and MCP endpoint support Rumble publishing, letting you trigger uploads programmatically from external tools or automations. See the [API & MCP docs](https://nushir.com/channels.html#api) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
