A well-maintained Facebook Page signals credibility — but keeping it active across posts, photos, videos, and Reels while staying on top of every other channel is a full-time job on its own. Nushir's AI agent manages your Facebook publishing end to end. Connect your Page once, tell the agent your tone and any restrictions, and it builds a monthly plan. Every piece of content lands in your calendar as a draft; link previews are pulled automatically, and nothing goes live until you approve it.

## Platform Specs

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Content types** | Page Posts, Photos, Video, Reels |
| **Text limit** | 63,206 characters |
| **Media** | Photo album or video |
| **Links** | Link preview image and description pulled automatically |

## What the Agent Does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plans your posting rhythm">
    The agent determines a sustainable posting frequency for your Facebook Page based on your audience size and content mix. It distributes topics across the month so your Page stays active without repeating the same format back to back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writes in your tone">
    Facebook's generous 63,206-character limit means there's room for longer narratives, context-rich updates, and detailed announcements. The agent writes each post in your voice — concise when the content calls for it, expansive when it doesn't — in Arabic or English, applying your content restrictions on every draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepares media">
    For photo posts the agent compiles an album from your media library; for video and Reels it attaches the file and writes the accompanying description. If your library doesn't have the right assets, the agent generates on-brand images. Link previews for URL posts are fetched and formatted automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reads results after publishing">
    After each post goes live, the agent reads your Facebook Page Insights — reach, reactions, shares, link clicks, best-performing times — and uses those signals to shape the next month's plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Connect Facebook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the channel">
    In your Nushir dashboard, click **Add Channel** and select **Facebook**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate with Meta">
    You'll be redirected to the Meta login flow. Sign in and select which Facebook Page you want to connect. Review the permissions list, then click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your tone and restrictions">
    Choose your language (Arabic or English), describe your brand voice, and add any topics or phrases the agent should avoid on this Page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review drafts in your calendar">
    Drafts appear in the content calendar. Click any draft to edit the text, swap media, or reschedule. Approve when ready — the post publishes at the time you set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can disconnect your Facebook Page at any time from **Channel Settings** in Nushir, or by removing Nushir from your Meta account's connected apps.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the agent post to Facebook without my approval?">
    No. Every draft defaults to **Pending** in your calendar. You can opt in to auto-publish for this channel individually from the channel settings page — it is never enabled by default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does each Facebook Page count as one channel slot?">
    Yes. Each connected Facebook Page uses one channel slot from your plan. Managing two Pages means two channel slots. See the [pricing page](https://nushir.com/pricing.html) for limits per plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish to Facebook via the API or MCP?">
    Yes. Nushir supports REST API and MCP publishing on all channels, including Facebook Pages. See the [API & MCP documentation](/channels/overview#custom-channel) for integration details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
