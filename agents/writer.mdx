The Writer agent fills every post slot with copy that sounds like you and fits the platform it will appear on. It reads your brand voice settings, studies the examples you upload, and applies what it learns to every draft it produces. You do not brief it on tone each time — it already knows.

<Tip>
  Upload 5–10 of your best past posts during [Brand Setup](/brand-setup) before you run the Writer for the first time. The more examples it has, the less editing you will need to do on its output.
</Tip>

## What the Writer Does

- **Learns from your archive** — the Writer reads your past posts, tone of voice description, and any vocabulary or style notes you provided during brand setup. It extracts patterns and applies them to every new draft.
- **Writes in Arabic and English** — Arabic is the Writer's first language, not a translation layer. It writes in Modern Standard Arabic or Gulf dialect per your settings, and carries the same tone into English when you need it.
- **Produces one idea in multiple formats** — the Writer does not write one generic post and copy it everywhere. It takes a single topic and produces the format that suits each channel: a four-post thread for X, a longer article for LinkedIn, a 15-second conversational script for Reels.
- **Respects your prohibited words list** — any words, phrases, or claims on your red-line list are treated as hard constraints. Drafts are automatically rewritten before they reach your calendar if they contain prohibited content.
- **Stays within platform character limits** — the Writer knows the hard and recommended limits for every supported platform and writes to them automatically.

## Language Support

The Writer supports Arabic and English natively. Configure your language preference per channel in **Settings → Channels → [Channel name] → Language**. Options include:

- **Arabic — Modern Standard (MSA)** — appropriate for formal content, news, articles, and cross-regional reach.
- **Arabic — Gulf dialect** — natural and conversational; works well for video scripts, social captions, and consumer brands in the GCC.
- **English** — same tone and brand voice as your Arabic content.
- **Mixed** — the Writer can open in one language and close in another, or produce a primary caption in Arabic with an English subtitle line, depending on what you specify.

## Platform Character Limits

The Writer applies these limits automatically. You do not need to count characters yourself.

| Platform      | Limit         | Notes                               |
|---------------|---------------|-------------------------------------|
| X (Twitter)   | 280 chars     | Thread posts are 280 chars each     |
| Instagram     | 2,200 chars   | First 125 chars show before "more"  |
| LinkedIn      | 3,000 chars   | Articles have a separate, higher limit |
| TikTok        | 2,200 chars   | Caption only; script is separate    |
| Facebook      | 63,206 chars  | Practical limit is much shorter     |
| Threads       | 500 chars     |                                     |
| Discord       | 2,000 chars   |                                     |
| Snapchat      | 250 chars     |                                     |
| Telegram      | 4,096 chars   |                                     |
| Pinterest     | 500 chars     | Pin description                     |

## How to Trigger the Writer

The Writer runs automatically after the Planner creates and you confirm a set of draft slots. You do not need to do anything — within a few minutes of confirming your plan, slots will have copy ready to review.

You can also trigger the Writer manually on any empty slot:

1. Open the slot in Calendar view.
2. Click **Write** in the post editor.
3. Optionally add a brief or adjust the topic before clicking **Generate**.

The Writer produces the draft and saves it to the slot. You can regenerate as many times as you like — only the version you approve is ever published.

## Editing Copy

Click any draft in the Calendar to open the post editor. Make any changes you want directly in the editor — rewrite sentences, adjust the call to action, change the language, or trim for length. Click **Save** when done.

Every edit you make is recorded as implicit tone feedback. The Writer compares its draft to your edited version and adjusts its future output accordingly. You do not need to update your brand settings manually — the edits themselves teach it.
