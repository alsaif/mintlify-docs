The Designer agent handles the visual side of every post. It reaches into your media library first, and only generates new visuals when you do not have a suitable asset on hand. Either way, everything it produces uses your brand colors, fonts, and logo — without you specifying them each time.

## What the Designer Produces

| Output type      | Description                                                                 |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Post images      | Single-image posts with your brand identity applied                         |
| Carousels        | Multi-slide image sets for Instagram and LinkedIn                           |
| Short video      | 15–60 second clips with Arabic voiceover and burned-in captions             |
| Stories          | Vertical full-screen frames for Instagram, Snapchat, and WhatsApp           |
| Product shots    | Clean product-focused frames sized for the feed                             |

## Output Sizes

The Designer produces every visual in all three standard crops by default:

- **1:1 square (1080 × 1080)** — feed posts on Instagram, Facebook, X, and Threads.
- **9:16 vertical (1080 × 1920)** — Reels, TikTok, Snapchat Stories, and Instagram Stories.
- **16:9 wide (1920 × 1080)** — YouTube thumbnails and covers, long-form video headers.

You do not need to request a specific size. When a post is assigned to multiple channels, the Designer prepares whichever crops each platform requires.

## How to Use the Designer

The Designer runs automatically after the Writer fills a slot. Once copy exists for a draft, the Designer attaches media without any action on your part.

You can also trigger it manually on any draft:

1. Open the draft in the Calendar.
2. Click **Design** in the post editor.
3. Review the media the Designer selected or generated.
4. Click **Regenerate** to get an alternative, or make manual adjustments in the editor.

## Your Media Library Comes First

The Designer checks your media library before generating anything. If you have a product photo, a team shot, or a brand video that fits the post, it uses that asset. AI generation only happens when there is nothing suitable in the library.

To keep the Designer working with your real content, keep your media library stocked. Upload assets in **Settings → Brand → Media Library**. Supported file types include JPG, PNG, SVG, MP4, and MOV.

<Note>
  **Video generation quotas apply by plan.** Studio includes 20 generated videos per month; Agency includes 60. When the monthly quota is reached, the Designer pauses video generation and logs the affected drafts. Scheduling and publishing for all other post types continues normally. You can purchase a 10-video add-on pack (SAR 49) at any time, or wait for the quota to reset at the start of the next month.
</Note>

## Short Video and Arabic Voiceover

When the Designer creates a short video, it can add an Arabic voiceover track synthesized from the Writer's script. Captions are burned into the video file itself so they display on every platform regardless of whether the viewer has sound on or subtitles enabled.

Video generation uses either:
- **Your own footage** from the media library, cut and captioned by the Designer.
- **Generated visuals** if no suitable footage is available, combined with your brand assets.

To enable Arabic voiceover on a video post, make sure the draft language is set to Arabic in the post editor before triggering the Designer.
