Nushir includes four agents that collaborate on the entire content lifecycle — from monthly planning to post-publish learning. Each agent owns one stage of a post's journey. You give them your goal and your brand voice once, and they come back with work ready for your review. No blank page. No missed posting slot.

## The Four Agents

<CardGroup cols={2}>

## How They Work Together

The four agents run in sequence on every post, but they share context with each other throughout the month.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Planner sets the schedule">
    You give the Planner a goal — for example, "Build awareness for the spring collection in March." It produces a full month of dated post slots distributed across your connected channels at a cadence that suits each platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writer fills the slots with copy">
    As soon as the Planner creates a slot, the Writer picks it up. It reads the topic, your brand voice settings, and the platform's requirements, then writes copy that fits. One idea becomes multiple formats — a thread for X, an article for LinkedIn, a 15-second script for Reels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Designer attaches media">
    The Designer takes the Writer's copy, checks your media library for relevant assets, and produces images or short video at the right size for the target platform. Your brand colors, fonts, and logo appear automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyst feeds results back into the next plan">
    After posts go live, the Analyst reads the engagement data and writes a plain-language summary of what worked. It adjusts post times for the following month and flags high-performing posts for evergreen reposting. The Planner reads these findings when building next month's calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You Stay in Control

The agents work ahead of you, but nothing reaches your audience without your sign-off.

- **Draft-first by default** — every post the agents create lands in your calendar as a draft.
- **Every post needs approval** — click Approve on a draft to schedule it for publishing. Unapproved posts stay in Draft indefinitely.
- **Edit any draft** — open any draft in the calendar editor, make changes, and save. Your edits are remembered as tone and style feedback for future posts.
- **Auto-publish is opt-in** — if you want the agents to publish without manual approval, you can enable auto-publish per channel in **Settings → Channels**. It is off by default on every channel.
