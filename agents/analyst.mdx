The Analyst agent reads what happens after you publish and feeds those findings back into everything that comes next. It does not hand you a spreadsheet and leave you to draw conclusions — it writes three clear observations each week in plain language, adjusts your schedule automatically, and builds a client-ready PDF report at the end of every month without any manual work from you.

<Tip>
  Connect all your channels before your first full month of publishing. The Analyst builds its timing and topic recommendations from patterns across your own accounts. The more channels it has access to, the more accurate and cross-channel its insights become.
</Tip>

## What the Analyst Does

- **Writes a weekly summary** — every week the Analyst produces three written observations about your content performance. These are plain-language findings, not a raw numbers dashboard. Example: "Your Tuesday posts on Instagram got 40% more saves this week — all three included a step-by-step format."
- **Learns from your account, not global averages** — the Analyst bases its timing and topic recommendations on your own audience's behaviour, not industry benchmarks. Your audience may be most active at times that differ significantly from platform-reported averages.
- **Adjusts post times automatically** — as the Analyst learns when your audience engages, it updates the default scheduling times for each channel. Changes take effect on the next plan the Planner builds.
- **Recommends evergreen reposts** — the Analyst flags posts with high engagement and suggests adding them to an Evergreen queue with a repeat cadence. Confirmed evergreen posts appear in your calendar each time they are scheduled to run.
- **Generates a monthly branded PDF report** — at the end of each month, the Analyst compiles a full performance report in your brand identity and sends it to you by email. The report is also available for download at any time from the Analytics tab.

## Where to Find Analytics

- **Analytics tab** — the main analytics view in the sidebar. Shows per-channel and per-post performance data, the weekly summary, evergreen recommendations, and all downloadable reports.
- **Calendar view** — each published post in the calendar shows inline engagement data (reach, engagement rate, saves, clicks) when you click on it. You can review performance without leaving the calendar.

## Monthly Report

The monthly report is generated automatically on the last day of each calendar month. It includes:

- A summary of the month's performance across all connected channels
- Top-performing posts by engagement rate, reach, and saves
- Time and day analysis showing when your audience was most active
- Channel-by-channel breakdown
- Recommended focus areas for the next month

The report is styled in your brand colors and fonts (as set in **Settings → Brand → Assets**) and arrives in your inbox as a PDF attachment. You can download it at any time from **Analytics → Reports**.

**Agency plan:** Separate reports are generated per client workspace. Each client receives a report in their own brand identity, not yours.

## Evergreen Recommendations

The Analyst continuously monitors your published posts for strong performance. When a post crosses a threshold for engagement relative to your channel's average, it appears in the **Evergreen** section of the Analytics tab with a suggested repost cadence.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the recommendation">
    Go to **Analytics → Evergreen**. The Analyst shows the post, its original performance, and the cadence it recommends (for example, "Repost every 90 days").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm or adjust the cadence">
    Accept the recommendation as-is, or change the repeat interval to suit your preference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Posts appear in your calendar">
    Confirmed evergreen posts are added to the Evergreen queue. Each scheduled run appears in your Calendar view so you always know what is going out and when. You can approve, edit, or remove individual instances at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
