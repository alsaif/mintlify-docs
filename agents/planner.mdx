The Planner agent converts a single goal into a complete monthly content calendar. Instead of staring at an empty grid and deciding what to post each day, you give the Planner a direction and it returns a realistic, distributed plan — topics assigned to channels, spread across the month at a cadence that will not overwhelm your audience or leave weeks silent.

## What the Planner Does

- **Builds a full month from one goal** — a single sentence like "launch product v2 on September 1" is enough to generate a complete calendar with topics, post types, and channel assignments for every slot.
- **Sets a realistic posting rhythm** — the Planner understands how much content each channel can support in a week. It will not schedule eight posts on Instagram in a single day, and it will not leave a channel untouched for three weeks.
- **Accounts for local seasons and holidays** — Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Saudi National Day, UAE National Day, and other regional dates are built into the Planner's calendar awareness. It structures content naturally around these periods rather than ignoring them.
- **Flags thin weeks before they happen** — if a week is lighter than the rest of the month, the Planner highlights it in the calendar view so you can add content before the gap appears to your audience.

## How to Use the Planner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new plan">
    Navigate to **Calendar** and click **New Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your goal">
    Type your goal for the month in plain language. Be as specific as you can — see the tip below.

    <Tip>
      Specific goals produce better plans. "Launch product v2 on Sep 1 with a three-week build-up on Instagram and LinkedIn" will generate a more targeted calendar than "post more content this month." Include your launch date, the channels you care about most, and any milestones.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark key dates (optional)">
    If your plan anchors around specific events — a product launch, a sale period, a regional holiday — mark them on the mini-calendar before generating. The Planner builds content clusters around these dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the plan">
    Click **Generate Plan**. The Planner produces the calendar in a few seconds and opens it in Calendar view, where you can see all proposed slots laid out by date and channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Read through the proposed slots. If the overall structure looks right, click **Confirm Plan** to convert all slots into drafts. The Writer and Designer pick up from here automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing the Plan

The Planner's output is a starting point, not a fixed schedule. You can adjust it freely before and after confirming:

- **Drag posts to different dates** — click and drag any slot in Calendar view to move it to a new date or time.
- **Delete unwanted slots** — hover over a slot and click the delete icon to remove it entirely.
- **Add manual slots** — click any empty cell in the calendar to create a new post slot and assign it to a channel and topic yourself.
- **Re-run the Planner** — if you want a completely different distribution, return to **New Plan** and generate again. Existing confirmed drafts are not affected.

<Note>
  The Planner creates empty draft slots with topic suggestions. The Writer and Designer still need to fill those slots with copy and media before they are ready to approve. On most plans, this happens automatically within a few minutes of confirming the plan.
</Note>
